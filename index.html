Interest is what accrued during each calendar month. Principal is payment minus interest; a negative number means the balance grew that month.

How the interest is figured

Daily rate is APR ÷ 365. Every day, payments and purchases that post that day change the balance, then that day's interest is added to it. At each statement close, the cycle's interest equals the average daily balance × daily rate × days in the cycle. Payments made mid-cycle lower every remaining day's balance, so paying earlier cuts interest.

A simple calculator charges APR ÷ 12 once a month on the balance. That ignores daily compounding and the days between the statement and your payment, so it comes out a bit low.
